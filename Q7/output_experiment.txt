PREDICTIONS (written before the experiment was run):
  P1. Clustered edits (bursts of 100 inserts): the gap buffer needs about N/3 element moves per burst instead of ~50*N, so moves fall by two orders of magnitude and time by at least 20x at N >= 100,000.
  P2. Burst length 1 (every insert at a new random place): no advantage - moves per insert about N/3 (gap) vs N/2 (ArrayList); times roughly equal.
  P3. Break-even around a burst length of 2-3 inserts; improvement grows roughly in proportion to the burst length.
  P4. get(i) stays O(1) but costs one extra comparison; memory: capacity slack of the gap buffer is similar to ArrayList.
